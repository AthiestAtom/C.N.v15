# Chandigarh calibration sources

## Traffic

1. Chandigarh Administration / Master Plan 2031 traffic chapter: RITES Comprehensive Mobility Plan survey. It reports 2009 V/C ratios for major roads and identifies Madhya Marg and Udyog Path as already above capacity in that historical survey.
2. RITES Primary Survey 2011, reproduced in the Chandigarh Metro Rail DPR traffic-demand chapter: 16 mid-block locations, 16-hour classified counts, PCU totals, morning/evening peaks and directional split.
3. 2018 MetroCount/Radar-Gun study of Chandigarh V-2 roads: Madhya Marg, Purv Marg, Dakshin Marg, Himalaya Marg, Jan Marg and Udyog Path; includes peak PCU/h, road lanes, design service volume, V/C, LOS and 85th-percentile speed.

## Air quality

CPCB NAAQM 2017 provides Chandigarh NO2 minimum, maximum and annual-average values for Modern Foods Industrial Area, Sector-17 C, Punjab Engineering College Sector-12, Sector-39 IMTECH and Kaimbwala Village.

## Important limitation

These are historical observations. They are valuable for calibration and historical validation, not a substitute for current 2026 sensor feeds. A production Chandigarh digital twin should ingest current ITMS/sensor observations when access is available and retain the historical datasets as a back-testing benchmark.
